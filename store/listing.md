## Full description (max 4000)

```
ScoreEase keeps score of anything — a table tennis rally, a family UNO night, a cricket match in the park or a whole sports day. Pick a game, add the players and tap to score. No sign-up, no ads.

WORKS OFFLINE
Everything is saved on your phone first. Nothing goes online until you choose to share, so it works on the court, in the park or on a flight.

READY-MADE GAMES
Start in seconds with templates for table tennis, badminton, volleyball, pickleball, cricket, basketball, football, rummy, UNO, golf, carrom, musical chairs, races and more. Or make your own with a simple counter or a round-by-round score sheet.

SCORING THAT FITS THE GAME
• Counters for quick tallies, with tap-to-add bursts
• Round-by-round score sheets for card and board games
• Sets and games with serve tracking for racket sports
• Overs, wickets and runs for cricket
• Places and points for races and heats

SHARE IT LIVE
Share a game with a link, a QR code or a 6-letter code. Friends follow the score live on their phone or the web, and you can add a password for viewing or for scoring.

TV MODE
Put the scoreboard on a big screen so everyone can see it.

EVENTS WITH TEAMS
Plan a games day: split people into teams, play several games and let ScoreEase add up the points for each place into a running team table.

NEVER LOSE A POINT
Every point is logged. Undo and redo mistakes, look back through the history, and see who scored.

PLAYERS AND STATS
See wins, games played and results for the people you play with most.

EXPORT AND BACK UP
Export results to CSV for a spreadsheet, share a result card, or back up your games.

LOOKS GOOD EVERYWHERE
Designed for phones, tablets and the web, in light and dark themes.

Perfect for families, clubs, coaches, schools, office tournaments and anyone who has ever lost track of the score.
```
