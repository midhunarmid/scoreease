ScoreEase Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

ScoreEase is a scorekeeping app for Android and the web, available at scoreease.armid.in. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes and the choices you have. In short: there is no sign-up, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your games stay on your device unless you choose to share one.

Data stored on your device

The games you create, the player and team names you type, scores, history, events and your settings (such as theme, vibration and keep-screen-on) are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes them.

Data sent when you share a game

Sharing is optional. When you share a game live, join a shared game by code, or open an old shared board, the app connects to Google Firebase:

  • Anonymous sign-in. Firebase Authentication gives your app a random anonymous ID. It is not linked to your name, email address, phone number or Google account. It is used only to know who owns a shared game and who has entered its password.
  • The shared game. The game's title and description, player and team names, settings and each score change are stored in the Firebase Realtime Database so others can follow along live. Each score change records the anonymous ID of the device that made it.
  • Passwords. If you protect a game with a password, only a one-way hash of it is stored. The password itself never leaves your device.

Anyone with the link, QR code or 6-letter code can view a shared game, unless you add a viewing password. Only enter names you are happy for those people to see.

Firebase is operated by Google, which may process technical data such as your IP address to provide the service. See Firebase privacy and security and the Google Privacy Policy.

Fonts

The app may download its fonts from Google Fonts the first time they are needed. This request does not include any of your game data. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, email addresses or phone numbers.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.
  • No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone.

Files you export or import

Backups, CSV exports and result cards are created on your device. They go only where you choose to send them, using your device's share sheet. Importing a backup reads the file you pick, on your device.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Local games: delete them in the app, or uninstall the app.
  • Shared games: the owner can stop sharing a game in the app, which deletes it, its scores and its password hashes from the server. Your local copy stays.
  • Saved passwords: use Settings → Forget saved passwords to remove them from the server.

If you can no longer reach a game you shared and want it removed, contact us with its code and we will delete it.

Security

Data sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Database security rules allow only the owner of a game, or someone with its password, to change it.

Children

ScoreEase is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It has no accounts, ads or chat. If a child's name was entered in a shared game, the game's owner can stop sharing it, or you can contact us to remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date above.

Contact
